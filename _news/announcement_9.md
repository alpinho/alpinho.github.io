You can view the slides of my talk last week at <a href="https://www.musicperception.org/program">SMPC 2026</a> conference in Evanston -- Chicago, titled <i>Shared and context-dependent cortico--striatal--cerebellar organization of temporal prediction</i>. In this talk, I presented our findings from a multi-task fMRI study showing that temporal prediction across different rhythmic structures, sensory modalities, and task demands is supported by a shared cortico--striatal--cerebellar architecture, while also revealing a specialized role of the dorsal striatum in beat-based processing and explicit motor timing. The slides are available <a href="https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/shared-and-context-dependent-cortico-striatal-cerebellar-organization-of-temporal-prediction/288836324">here</a>.
